Instagram Analyzer: setup guide

Every morning it looks at the Instagram accounts you choose and finds the posts that are working. It suggests ideas for your own posts and shows how your posts perform. Setup takes about 45 minutes, once.

What you need

  • A Mac connected to the internet.
  • Your Instagram account set as a Business or Creator account, linked to a Facebook Page (step 3 shows how).
  • Your Meta developer account (developers.facebook.com).
  • An account with one AI company: OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini).
  • Optional: a Gmail address, if you want the daily email.

Step 1: Install

  1. Open the analyzer folder you were sent (usually in Downloads).
  2. Double-click Install.command.
  3. If your Mac says it "could not verify" the file, click Done. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to "Install.command", then Open Anyway again.
  4. If it asks whether Terminal may access your Downloads folder, click Allow.
  5. A black Terminal window shows progress. Wait until it says ✅ Installed (2–5 minutes). A folder called Instagram Analyzer then opens. It lives in your home folder.

After that the analyzer runs by itself every day at 8:00. If your Mac is asleep at 8:00, it runs when the Mac wakes. If the Mac is shut down, that day is skipped.

Step 2: Open your dashboard

  1. In the Instagram Analyzer folder, double-click Start.command. Your browser opens the dashboard.
  2. Keep the Terminal window open while you use the dashboard. Close it when you're done.
  3. Tip: drag Start.command onto your Dock so it's one click next time.
  4. Click Settings at the top. You'll fill this page in over the next steps.

Step 3: Connect Instagram

A. Make your Instagram a professional account (skip if it already is)

  1. In the Instagram app, go to your profile, then ☰ menu → Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account. Choose Business or Creator.
  2. Link it to a Facebook Page: Edit profile → Page → Connect, or create a Page there.

B. Create your Meta app

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps and click Create app.
  2. Give it any name, for example "My Analyzer". If asked for a use case, choose Other. If asked for an app type, choose Business.
  3. In the app dashboard, find Instagram in the list of products and click Set up. If you see a choice, pick the option that mentions Facebook Login.
  4. In the left menu, open App settings → Basic. Copy the App ID, then click Show next to App Secret and copy that too. Paste both into a note for now.

C. Get an access token

  1. Open the Graph API Explorer.
  2. On the right, set Meta App to the app you just made.
  3. Under Permissions, add each of these: instagram_basic instagram_manage_insights pages_show_list pages_read_engagement business_management
  4. Click Generate Access Token. Log in, and tick your Facebook Page and your Instagram account when asked.
  5. Copy the long token that appears in the Access Token box.

D. Paste into Settings

  1. In Settings → 3. Instagram connection, paste the App ID, the App Secret and the token.
  2. Leave "Where posts come from" on Meta first (free). Personal (non-business) accounts can't be read through Meta. If you watch some, you can add an Apify token later and it will read just those.
  3. Click Save settings. You should see "Connected to Instagram @yourhandle". The connection doesn't expire, so you only do this once.
Meta changes these screens often. If a button has a slightly different name, look for the closest match. The three things you need are always the same: App ID, App Secret and an access token with those five permissions.

Step 4: Add your AI key

  1. Pick one company and open its key page:
    • OpenAI: platform.openai.com/api-keys
    • Anthropic: console.anthropic.com/settings/keys
    • Google: aistudio.google.com/apikey
  2. Create a new key and copy it. Add some credit on that company's billing page; $10 is plenty to start. Also set a monthly spending limit there so costs never surprise you.
  3. In Settings → 2. AI provider, choose the same company, paste the key and click Save settings.

Step 5: Daily email (optional)

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. If it says the setting isn't available, turn on 2-Step Verification first.
  2. Name it "Analyzer" and click Create. Copy the 16-letter password.
  3. In Settings → 4. Daily email, tick the box, enter your Gmail address, paste the password and click Save settings.

Step 6: Choose accounts and results

  1. Settings → 1. Your brand: check the name, handle and description. The AI uses these to judge what fits you.
  2. Settings → 5. Accounts to watch: one Instagram handle per line. It starts with a suggested list. Add or remove handles any time. Only Business or Creator accounts can be read; personal accounts are skipped.
  3. Settings → 6. Results: how far back to look, how many posts to check and how many ideas you get each day. Bigger numbers cost a little more AI credit.
  4. Click Save settings.

Step 7: First run

  1. Go back to the dashboard and click Run analysis now.
  2. Wait 5–15 minutes, then refresh the page. You'll see top posts, and Today's ideas & report will have fresh ideas.
  3. If the top of the dashboard shows ❌, see "If something goes wrong" below.

Every day

  1. Read the morning email, or open the dashboard and click Today's ideas & report.
  2. Make and post the ideas you like from your phone, as you normally would.
  3. The next day, open the dashboard. In Your recent posts, choose the idea each post came from in the dropdown next to it. That's how the dashboard can tell suggested posts from the rest.
  4. Check Daily and Weekly. "Suggested vs normal" above 1.0× means posts from suggested ideas are beating your usual engagement.

If something goes wrong

  • ❌ collected 0 posts: redo Step 3 C–D to reconnect Instagram. Check that the accounts you watch are Business or Creator accounts.
  • No ideas appear: check the AI key and that the account has credit (Step 4).
  • No email: create a new Gmail app password (Step 5) and save it again.
  • Dashboard won't open: double-click Start.command again. Your browser should open http://127.0.0.1:8765.
  • For a detailed record to send to whoever helps you, open the file logs/run.log inside the Instagram Analyzer folder.

Removing it

Drag the Instagram Analyzer folder from your home folder to the Trash.
